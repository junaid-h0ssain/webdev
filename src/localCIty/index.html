Jamburi park
A lively park in the heart of Agrabad, the financial district of Chittagong, Jamburi Park is a popular spot for both locals and tourists. It features beautifully landscaped gardens, walking paths, and a variety of recreational facilities including playgrounds and picnic areas. The park is especially vibrant during weekends and holidays when families gather to enjoy the outdoors.
